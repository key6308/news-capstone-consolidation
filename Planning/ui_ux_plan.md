# UI/UX Plan

- **Home:** approved article feed with headline, author/publisher, date, and short preview.
- **Article page:** readable headline, publication information, and article body.
- **Reader subscriptions:** separate multi-select controls for publishers and journalists.
- **Journalist workflow:** submit article, see approval state, and manage owned newsletters.
- **Editor workflow:** review pending articles and use one clear approval action.
- **Newsletters:** list curated newsletters and links to their approved articles.
- **Navigation:** role-specific links are displayed only when relevant.
- **Responsive layout:** simple CSS flex layout adapts to smaller screens.
